How the service works

  1. Submit a plant and a destination

    The plant name as it appears in your catalogue, and the buyer's country and region.

  2. The name is matched

    Against scientific names, synonyms and common names, tolerating minor misspellings. Ambiguous names return suggestions to choose from rather than a guess.

  3. The regulation record is returned

    Whether the species is listed in that jurisdiction, with the regulating authority, its classification and the data release used. 

POST /v1/regulatory-check
{
  "plant_query": "Cortaderia jubata",
  "ship_to": { "country": "US", "region": "CA" }
}
Scope

The service reports regulation records. It does not determine whether a plant may legally be bought, sold or shipped; that decision remains with your compliance team and the relevant authority.

What the service provides

  • Screening of plant listings before checkout, catalogue approval or shipment.
  • Matching that handles the names plants are actually sold under.
  • A record behind every result: the authority, its classification and notes, and the release.
  • Versioned data releases, documented on the Method page.
