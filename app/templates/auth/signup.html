Full Access for Researchers

Free website access to full jurisdiction lists for research, education, extension, regulation, farming and public-interest use.

What full access includes

  • The complete list of regulated plants for every jurisdiction on the map.
  • Regulation details on each species page: the jurisdictions, regulating authorities and classifications.
  • The same data releases used across the site, documented on the Method page.

Without an account, the map shows a sample of each list and species pages show how many jurisdictions regulate a plant, but not which.

How access works

  1. Request access

    Tell us who you are, your organisation, and how you plan to use the data.

  2. We review your request

    Each request is reviewed individually, and we email you when it has been approved.

  3. Sign in with an email link

    There is no password: approved accounts sign in with a one-time link sent by email.
